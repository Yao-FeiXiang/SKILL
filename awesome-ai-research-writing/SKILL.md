# Awesome AI Research Writing

This skill wraps the prompt and workflow collection from
`Leey21/awesome-ai-research-writing`.

## When To Use

Use this skill for research-paper writing tasks such as:
- translating Chinese drafts into English academic LaTeX or Word text
- translating English LaTeX into Chinese for comprehension
- polishing Chinese or English paper prose
- shortening or expanding manuscript paragraphs
- checking argument logic and reviewer-facing weaknesses
- reducing obvious AI-generated style
- writing figure or table captions
- analyzing experiments and results
- reviewing a full manuscript from a reviewer perspective
- recommending academic plotting styles or model choices

## Source Material

Read `README.md` in this directory and select the relevant section for the
user's task. Preserve the output constraints from that section, especially
whether the target is LaTeX, Word/plain text, Chinese, or English.

When the user asks to apply one of the templates, do the work directly using
the relevant section's role, constraints, output format, and self-check rules.
Do not paste the template back unless the user explicitly asks for the prompt
itself.

## Section Map

- `中转英-latex`: Chinese draft to English academic LaTeX.
- `英转中-latex`: English LaTeX to readable Chinese.
- `中转英-word`: Chinese draft to English academic prose for Word.
- `中转中-word`: Chinese draft to formal Chinese academic prose for Word.
- `缩写`: lightly shorten English LaTeX while preserving meaning.
- `扩写`: lightly expand English LaTeX while preserving meaning.
- `表达润色（英文论文）`: polish English paper prose.
- `表达润色（中文论文）`: polish Chinese paper prose.
- `逻辑检查`: identify logic gaps and argument weaknesses.
- `去 AI 味（LaTeX 英文）`: make English LaTeX prose more natural.
- `去 AI 味（Word 中文）`: make Chinese Word prose more natural.
- `论文架构图`: produce paper architecture diagram guidance.
- `实验绘图推荐`: recommend experiment plotting approaches.
- `生成图的标题`: write figure captions.
- `生成表的标题`: write table captions.
- `实验分析`: analyze experimental results.
- `论文整体以 Reviewer 视角进行审视`: full-paper reviewer critique.
- `模型选择`: choose suitable models for writing tasks.
